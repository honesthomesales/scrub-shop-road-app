# How to Clear Cache and Get Fresh Version - Android Chrome

## Quick Steps to Get the Latest Version

### Method 1: Hard Refresh (Easiest)

1. **Open Chrome on your Android phone**
2. **Go to your app:** `https://honesthomesales.github.io/scrub-shop-road-app`
3. **Long-press the refresh button** (circular arrow icon in address bar)
4. **Tap "Hard Reload"** or "Empty Cache and Hard Reload"
5. **Wait for page to reload**

### Method 2: Clear Site Data

1. **Open Chrome on your Android phone**
2. **Go to your app:** `https://honesthomesales.github.io/scrub-shop-road-app`
3. **Tap the lock icon** (or "i" icon) in the address bar
4. **Tap "Site settings"**
5. **Tap "Clear & Reset"**
6. **Tap "Clear & Reset" again to confirm**
7. **Go back and reload the page**

### Method 3: Clear All Chrome Data (Nuclear Option)

1. **Open Chrome Settings:**
   - Tap 3 dots menu (top right)
   - Tap "Settings"
2. **Go to Privacy:**
   - Tap "Privacy and security"
   - Tap "Clear browsing data"
3. **Select what to clear:**
   - ✅ Check "Cached images and files"
   - ✅ Check "Cookies and site data" (optional)
   - ⚠️ Uncheck everything else (to keep your history)
4. **Select time range:**
   - Choose "Last hour" or "Last 24 hours"
5. **Tap "Clear data"**
6. **Go back to your app and reload**

### Method 4: Use Incognito Mode (Always Fresh)

1. **Open Chrome**
2. **Tap 3 dots menu** → **"New incognito tab"**
3. **Visit:** `https://honesthomesales.github.io/scrub-shop-road-app`
4. **This always loads fresh** (no cache)

## How to Verify You Have the Latest Version

### Look for This in the Green Banner:

After the page loads, wait 3-5 seconds. If you see a **green banner** at the bottom, it should say:

```
App is Installable
Tap Chrome menu (3 dots) → "Install app"
Version: PWA-v2 (Updated)  ← Look for this!
```

**If you see "Version: PWA-v2 (Updated)"** = You have the latest version! ✅

**If you don't see the green banner at all** = You might have an old version (clear cache)

## Still Not Working?

If you still don't see the green banner after clearing cache:

1. **Try incognito mode** (Method 4) - This always works
